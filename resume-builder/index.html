Summary

A resume builder that doesn’t hold your PDF hostage

Most online resume builders let you type everything in, then stop you at the download button: create an account, start a “free” trial, or pay to remove a watermark. This one doesn’t. The PDF download is free, every time, with no account and no watermark.

It works differently under the hood, too. The form, the preview, and the PDF are all produced inside your browser. Nothing you type — your name, address, phone number, or work history — is ever sent to a server. There is no server to send it to: this page loads no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party code at all.

ATS-friendly by design

Many employers run resumes through applicant tracking software before a person reads them. Decorative two-column layouts often scramble in those systems. The Classic template here is a single column with real text and real headings in reading order — the layout that parses reliably. The PDF contains selectable text, not a picture of text, so it can be read by software and by people.

Your data stays yours

Your draft autosaves to this browser’s local storage so a refresh never loses work, and the “Clear my data” button removes it whenever you choose. You can also export your resume as a small .json file — a copy you own — and import it later, or on another device, to keep editing. On a shared or public computer, export your file and clear your data when you finish.

FAQ

Is there a free resume builder online with no sign-up?
This is one. It runs online in your browser, there is no account or email address to hand over, and the PDF download is free and unwatermarked. Nothing is uploaded — your resume stays in this browser until you clear it.
Is the PDF download really free?
Yes. No account, no trial, no watermark, no paywall. The site runs on optional tips.
Is my resume uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything runs in your browser. This page makes no network requests with your data — you can verify that in your browser’s developer tools.
Will it pass applicant tracking systems?
The Classic template is single-column real text — the layout that parses most reliably. No layout can guarantee any particular system, but this avoids the common failure modes.
How do I save my resume?
It autosaves in this browser. For a copy you own, use Export .json — importing it later restores everything exactly.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes — the editor and preview both work on mobile. PDF saving works in mobile browsers’ print/share flow.
